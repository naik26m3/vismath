What min-h-0 does

A flex child will not go smaller than its content unless you allow it. min-h-0 is how you allow it.

The black frame is the screen: 320px tall, and it never changes. Inside is a title and a blue box holding tall content. Tick the box and watch the blue box.

The rule: for a box to scroll, every flex box between it and the thing with a fixed height needs min-h-0. Miss one and that box grows to fit its content, and everything inside it grows too.
